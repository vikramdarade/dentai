# Intent: DentAI Growth Loop, Interactive Demo Tuning, & Practice Monetization

**Status:** Confirmed & Approved
**Model:** Product-Led Growth (Bottom-Up Solo Freemium -> Paid Practice Tier)

---

## 1. Context & Objective
DentAI has completed its core clinical scribing engine (8 procedure templates, ADA billing codes, multi-tier fallback resilience) and multi-clinic architecture (invite codes, member approvals, cross-clinic switcher).

The next milestone is aligning the **Interactive Demo** (`#/demo`) and **Outreach Strategy** with an organic, peer-to-peer growth loop that drives dentist-to-dentist referrals and converts practice owners into paying customers.

---

## 2. Statement of Intent

- **Outcome:** 
  1. Retune the interactive product demo to emphasize clinical and financial ROI (1.5–2 hours saved per day per chair), showcase the organic peer-sharing moment (templates and invite codes), and present clear Free Solo vs. Paid Practice tiers.
  2. Overhaul the outreach playbook (`OUTREACH_CAMPAIGN.md`) to replace legacy banking/APRA text with authentic, peer-to-peer dental growth scripts (study clubs, CPD groups, locum shift handovers, and associate-to-owner pitches).
- **User:**
  - *Free Solo Dentists & Locums:* Bottom-up advocates who experience chairside relief and share templates/codes with peers.
  - *Paying Practice Owners:* Commercial buyers who pay for practice-wide compliance, multi-chair oversight, central audit logs, and standardized clinic templates.
- **Why Now:**
  The technology is stable and tested; growth and monetization require conversion-focused presentation and authentic clinical distribution channels.
- **Success Criteria:**
  - Dentists watching the demo immediately recognize the chairside time savings and see how easy it is to share templates with colleagues.
  - Practice owners watching the demo see the financial and risk-mitigation value of the paid multi-chair plan.
  - Outreach copy provides ready-to-use, professional peer-share messages for WhatsApp, SMS, study clubs, and locum handoffs.
- **Constraints:**
  - No gimmicky marketing language or spam-like referral mechanics. Professional clinical credibility must remain paramount.
  - Zero disruption to existing clinical scribing or database safety.
- **Out of Scope (Immediate Phase):**
  - Full Stripe/payment gateway implementation (pricing and plan comparison will be presented clearly in UI, but payments are wired in a subsequent phase).
  - Unrelated infrastructure changes.

---

## 3. Core Monetization Architecture

| Feature / Capability | Free Solo Tier (Clinician) | Paid Practice Tier (Clinic Owner) |
|---|:---:|:---:|
| **Target Audience** | Individual Dentists, Locums, Associates | Multi-chair Practices, Clinic Owners, Group Practices |
| **Price Point** | Free (Forever) | $99 – $149 / month per clinic / chair |
| **Ambient Scribing** | Included (Standard Daily Allocation) | Unlimited High-Priority Hosted AI |
| **Offline Resilience** | Included (Zero data loss) | Included |
| **Procedure Templates** | Standard 8 Templates | Custom Practice Templates & Clinic Governance |
| **Practice Management** | Solo Clinic Only | Multi-Clinic Roll-up & Centralized Oversight |
| **Audit & Compliance** | Personal Session History | Clinic-Wide Immutable Audit Trail & Practitioner Attribution |
| **PMS Smart Copy** | Included | Included + Practice-wide PMS format presets |
| **Referral / Sharing** | Shareable Templates & Locum Codes | Practice Invite Codes with Member Approvals |
