# Trojan Horse: The Zero-Admin Operatory Wedge

## Problem Statement
How might we enable a solo associate dentist to start using DentAI in 30 seconds with zero IT permissions, leaving the office at 5:01 PM with zero homework—sparking peer envy that pulls Chair 2 and the clinic owner into a practice-wide subscription?

## Recommended Direction
**The "Web-Only Personal Speed Weapon + Lateral Staff Relay" Hybrid:**
1. **Day 1 (The Solo Wedge):** Associate opens `app.dentai.com` in their operatory browser. No downloads, no Windows admin rights. They scan the QR code with their phone to connect the microphone (`PhoneBeaconMode`). They paste notes into their PMS via `F12` / `Copy`.
2. **Day 3 (The Staff Infection):** The assistant leaves on time every day. DentAI generates the 1-click `[FRONT DESK ACTION ITEM]` slip for reception, eliminating billing confusion. The assistant invites the hygienist in Chair 2.
3. **Week 3 (The Commercial Lock):** 2 of 4 chairs are active. The clinic owner sees an extra $12,000 in booked treatment and pristine patient reviews. The owner claims the clinic workspace to unlock centralized multi-chair analytics and staff management.

## Key Assumptions to Validate
- [ ] **Assumption 1 (Zero-Admin Web Usability):** An associate can open the app and copy their first note into Dentrix/Eaglesoft in under 90 seconds without triggering any IT or browser security warnings.
- [ ] **Assumption 2 (Lateral Viral Pull):** Dental assistants who experience DentAI will actively advocate for Chair 2 and Chair 3 to adopt it within 10 days.
- [ ] **Assumption 3 (Owner Conversion Velocity):** Practice owners will upgrade to a paid clinic tier once 2+ chairs are active, driven by the Unbooked Treatment Pipeline report.

## MVP Scope (What's In)
- **Zero-Install Web Experience:** Instant access via browser; zero native installers or admin permissions.
- **QR Phone-as-Mic Pairing:** Instant beamforming phone microphone connection via camera scan (`PhoneBeaconMode`).
- **28px Ghost Micro-Pill & Hotkeys:** Unobtrusive floating bar with 1-stroke `F12` PMS clipboard copy in `SidebarDockMode.tsx`.
- **Guest Handoff Link:** A lightweight, read-only 3-item reception manifest URL for front-desk checkout.
- **"Gift Chair 2" Viral Invite:** 1-click invite code that gives a colleague 30 days of free access.
- **5:01 PM Clean Slate Badge:** Shareable milestone card celebrating zero uncompleted charts in `SpeedReviewStrip.tsx`.

## Not Doing (and Why)
- **No Native Desktop Windows Daemon (`.exe` / `.msi`):** Banned. Requires Windows Administrator passwords and triggers corporate IT antivirus reviews, killing Day 1 adoption.
- **No Chrome Extension Requirement:** Banned. Many clinical browser profiles lock extension installation. Pure web clipboard APIs are 100% permissionless.
- **No Top-Down Outbound Enterprise Sales:** Banned. High CAC and 6-month sales cycles. Growth must be 100% bottom-up product-led.
- **No In-App Credit Card Walls for Associates:** Banned. Associates will not input personal credit cards for practice-related workflows. First chair is frictionless.
